About SmartAIthon

The AI for Smart & Sustainable Cities Hackathon 2026 is a multi-stage innovation challenge organized to bring together students, developers, and innovators to solve real-world urban problems using Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies.
🚦
Smart Traffic
AI-driven signal control to reduce congestion and optimize urban flow.
🌍
Pollution Monitoring
Real-time air & water quality tracking for healthier city environments.
♻️
Waste Management
Smart collection routes and recycling systems powered by AI.
🚨
Disaster Prediction
Early-warning AI models to predict and mitigate urban disasters.
🛡️
Urban Safety
Intelligent surveillance and crowd analytics for safer public spaces.
