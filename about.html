About SmartAIthon 2026

The AI for Smart & Sustainable Cities Hackathon 2026 is a multi-stage innovation challenge designed to bring together students, developers, and innovators to solve real-world urban problems using Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies.

Participants will identify pressing challenges in areas such as traffic management, pollution monitoring, waste management, urban safety, and disaster prediction while developing innovative and scalable AI-driven solutions.

The hackathon focuses on creativity, technical implementation, real-world impact, and scalability, encouraging participants to transform innovative ideas into practical solutions for smarter and more sustainable cities.

This event provides an opportunity to showcase innovation, gain recognition, collaborate with talented participants, and build impactful AI solutions capable of addressing real urban challenges.

Problems We Address

Tackling the most pressing urban challenges with AI-powered solutions

🚦

Traffic Management

Develop intelligent traffic flow optimization systems to reduce congestion, minimize commute times, and enhance road safety through real-time data analysis and predictive algorithms.

🌍

Pollution Monitoring

Create AI-driven environmental monitoring solutions to track air quality, detect pollution sources, and provide actionable insights for cleaner urban environments.

♻️

Waste Management

Innovate smart waste collection and recycling systems using IoT sensors and machine learning to optimize routes, reduce costs, and improve sustainability.

🚨

Disaster Prediction

Build early warning systems and predictive models to anticipate natural disasters, enabling timely evacuations and emergency response coordination.

🛡️

Urban Safety

Design intelligent surveillance and emergency response systems to enhance public safety, detect potential threats, and improve law enforcement efficiency.

Hackathon Focus Areas

What we value and look for in innovative solutions

💡

Creativity

We encourage out-of-the-box thinking and novel approaches to solving complex urban challenges through creative AI applications.

⚙️

Technical Implementation

Strong emphasis on robust, scalable, and efficient technical solutions that demonstrate advanced AI and machine learning capabilities.

🌟

Real-World Impact

Solutions should address genuine urban problems with measurable benefits and potential for practical implementation in cities.

📈

Scalability

Projects should be designed with scalability in mind, capable of expanding from prototype to city-wide deployment.
