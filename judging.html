Judging Criteria

Understand how your projects will be evaluated in SmartAIthon 2026

Evaluation Criteria

Projects will be evaluated based on the following weighted criteria

25%
💡

Innovation

Originality and creativity of the solution, novelty of approach, and innovative use of AI technologies to address urban challenges.

25%
⚙️

Technical Implementation

Quality of code, technical complexity, proper use of AI/ML algorithms, and overall technical excellence of the implementation.

20%
🌟

Impact

Potential real-world impact on smart cities, scalability of the solution, and ability to address significant urban problems.

15%
📈

Feasibility

Practicality of implementation, cost-effectiveness, sustainability, and viability of the solution in real-world scenarios.

15%
🎤

Presentation

Clarity of presentation, quality of demonstration, ability to communicate ideas effectively, and overall presentation skills.

Rules & Regulations

All participants must adhere to the following rules and regulations throughout the hackathon:

  • Plagiarism Policy: Any form of plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification. All work must be original.
  • Original Work Only: Submissions must be the original work of team members. No third-party copyrighted material without proper attribution.
  • Deadline Adherence: Late submissions will not be accepted under any circumstances. Plan your time accordingly.
  • Judges' Decision: The decision of the judging panel will be final and binding on all participants.
  • Deadline Compliance: Teams must follow all deadlines strictly as mentioned in the timeline.
  • Code of Conduct: All participants must maintain professional conduct throughout the hackathon.
  • Submission Format: All submissions must follow the specified format and guidelines.
  • Team Participation: Only registered team members are allowed to participate and present.
  • Intellectual Property: Participants retain ownership of their intellectual property.
  • Documentation: Proper documentation is mandatory for all submissions.

Evaluation Process

Understanding how your project will be evaluated through different stages

📝

Initial Screening

All submissions undergo initial screening for completeness and compliance with guidelines before proceeding to evaluation.

👥

Expert Review

Qualified submissions are reviewed by industry experts and academic professionals based on the evaluation criteria.

🎯

Technical Assessment

Technical evaluation of code quality, implementation, and functionality of the submitted projects.

🏆

Final Selection

Finalists are selected based on cumulative scores and invited for presentation to the judging panel.
